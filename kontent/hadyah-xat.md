**Tekshir nima?**

## English (agar inglizcha so'rashsa)

**Subject:** Tekshir — from Techstars Startup Week: partnership as a brand

Assalomu alaykum!

My name is Abdulhamid. I presented my project **Tekshir** at Techstars Startup Week, where I met the Hadyah team. I loved the idea of growing a brand through Muslim creators, and we have registered on Hadyah as a brand. Now we would like to discuss a partnership in detail.

**What is Tekshir?** A free app that protects people from scams. In Uzbekistan people lose money and Telegram accounts every day through messages like "Your card is blocked, tell us the SMS code", "You won!", "Photos.apk" or "Vote for me". Most often the victims are our parents and grandparents. Tekshir recognizes these messages and warns in simple language.

**What it does today:** checks SMS, Telegram and WhatsApp messages automatically and on-device; detects fake Click, Payme and bank links; checks APK files before installation; finds dangerous installed apps; warns during calls from reported numbers; **family protection**, which alerts a relative on Telegram when a risk is found on a grandparent's phone; a phone security score (0–100). There is also a Telegram bot and a web app: tekshir.ah-digital.uz.

**Coming soon:** screenshot check, an "I was scammed — what now?" step-by-step guide, a Telegram account security check, QR code check, a "Spot the scammer" family quiz, a browser extension for computers and an iPhone version.

**Privacy first:** Tekshir does not store message text; phone and card numbers are stored only as hashes. No ads.

**Status:** preparing for the Google Play launch. The developer account is being verified, and testers for the closed test are ready.

**Why Hadyah:** Tekshir's message is "Protect your loved ones from scams". It is about family, honesty and caring for each other, which fits creators who talk about family and halal living.

**Our plan:** after the Google Play launch, a small pilot with **3–5 Uzbek-speaking creators in Uzbekistan**, aiming for app installs and scam awareness.
